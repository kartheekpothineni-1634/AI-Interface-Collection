## What is an AI Summary Card?

An AI Summary Card presents a condensed version of a long document, article, report, or other content.

The goal is to help users understand the main information without reading the entire source.

## Common Uses

AI summary interfaces are commonly used in:

- Research applications
- News platforms
- Document management tools
- Productivity applications
- Educational platforms
- AI assistants
- Knowledge management systems

## Why is it relevant?

People increasingly interact with large amounts of digital information.

AI-powered summarization helps users quickly identify important information and decide whether they need to read the full content.
